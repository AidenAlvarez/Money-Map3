The Problem

People, including college students, are not good at keeping budgets.

College students often have to balance tuition, food, transportation, entertainment, subscriptions, and other expenses. Without an easy way to see where their money is going, it can be difficult to stay within a budget and avoid overspending.

Research & Supporting Data

Money Map was developed around the idea that budgeting can be difficult when people do not have a simple way to track spending and compare it with their goals. The project combines secondary research with primary research from a student-focused survey.

This section intentionally focuses on the research collected for the Money Map project. Additional outside statistics can be added later if needed.

What the survey suggests: Most respondents showed interest in a budgeting app, while a large majority had never used a money-management app before. The results support continuing to investigate whether a simple, student-focused budgeting tool could address a real need.

The Money Map Solution

Money Map is a proposed budgeting app designed with college students in mind. Its goal is to make budgeting simple by helping users see their income, spending, budget limits, and savings goals in one place.

The survey results will be used to improve the idea and determine which features students would actually find useful.

Potential Features

Income Tracking

Record money coming in from jobs, allowances, scholarships, or other sources.

Spending Tracker

Organize purchases into categories so users can understand where their money goes.

Budget Goals

Set weekly or monthly spending limits and monitor progress.

Savings Goals

Create goals and track progress toward planned purchases or savings targets.

Simple Dashboard

See the most important financial information without complicated screens.

Student Focus

Design the experience around common college expenses and student budgeting habits.
